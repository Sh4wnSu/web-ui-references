# Apple-Inspired Web UI Design Philosophy

1. **Content First.** Prioritize clarity, usability, and functionality over decoration. Simplify complexity without sacrificing capability.

2. **System Consistency.** Design from the whole to the parts. Use shared design tokens, reusable components, and consistent visual and interaction patterns.

3. **Visual Hierarchy.** Establish hierarchy through typography, spacing, alignment, scale, and whitespace rather than excessive borders or containers.

4. **Geometric Precision.** Use coordinated proportions, continuous corners, concentric shapes, consistent icon silhouettes, and optical alignment.

5. **Color Restraint.** Use neutral foundations, purposeful accent colors, semantic colors, and context-aware palettes. Avoid arbitrary gradients and excessive saturation.

6. **Material and Depth.** Separate content, controls, and floating layers. Use transparency, glass, shadows, and elevation to express spatial hierarchy, not decoration.

7. **Natural Interaction.** Provide immediate feedback, direct manipulation, continuous transitions, and physically coherent motion. Keep animations purposeful, interruptible, and reversible.

8. **Usability and Performance.** Ensure accessibility, predictable behavior, user control, responsive layouts, and smooth rendering. Never sacrifice usability for aesthetics.

9. **Self-Explanatory Layout.** Avoid unnecessary secondary labels and descriptions. Let the layout do the explaining.

10. **Concise UI Copy.** Do not expose prompts, development notes, or implementation details in the user interface. Keep all user-facing text as concise as possible.
